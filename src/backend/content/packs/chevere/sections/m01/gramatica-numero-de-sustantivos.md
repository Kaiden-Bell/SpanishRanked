# Gramática: Número de sustantivos

## Objetivos

- Change nouns from singular to plural

## Número de los sustantivos (*Number of Nouns*)

How to make a noun plural:

- If a word ends in a vowel, add **–s** (cuaderno – cuaderno**s**) (chica – chica**s**)
- If a word ends in a consonant, add an **–es** (marcador – marcador**es**) (reloj – reloj**es**)
- If a word ends in **-z**, change the “z” to **c** and add **–es** (lápiz- lápic**es**) (luz – luc**es**)

The plural of the feminine singular article “la” (*the*) is therefore “las”. The plural of the masculine singular article “el” (*the*) is irregular: “los”.
